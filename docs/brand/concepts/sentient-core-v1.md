# Friday 智能核心 Logo 方向

## 用户明确的产品定位

产品是类似钢铁侠中的 JARVIS / FRIDAY 的人工智能助理：具备环境感知、判断、执行与系统协调能力。「对话驱动」不是用户认可的品牌定位，后续设计不应继续以聊天气泡为主符号。此前黑白、机器人脸、F 字母等要求也已被用户明确取消。Logo 必须具有科技感、传播性、记忆点和可解释的设计语言，使用图片模型生成。

## 本稿设计语言

以一个明亮菱形核心作为视线焦点，三片厚实几何结构构成围合外形。向心构图表达集中的智能与秩序；蓝色、冷银和青色核心建立科幻系统气质。中央核心可作为待命、感知、执行状态动效的延展元素，但动效尚未制作。

本稿为内置 imagegen 生成的 PNG 概念图，未用 SVG 绘制，未替换现有产品图标。材质与体积感较明显，后续应用图标与小尺寸版本仍需简化和检查；品牌记忆度尚未测试，也未获得用户确认。

## 完整提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create a new iconic premium logo for FRIDAY, an autonomous personal artificial intelligence assistant analogous in product role to JARVIS or FRIDAY from Iron Man: a perceptive, capable, always-present intelligence that understands the environment, coordinates systems and acts for its owner. This is NOT a chatbot or messaging app.
Design language: SENTIENT CORE. Exactly three large sculpted geometric vanes interlock around one luminous cyan central aperture, forming a compact powerful almost triangular circular emblem. The central negative-space aperture is a distinctive upright softly chamfered diamond, approximately one quarter of the emblem width, containing a small bright cyan solid diamond core with a generous dark gap around it. Three outer vanes are broad, simple, consistent in thickness and cut angle, arranged with controlled rotational balance. Their tips have crisp 45-degree cuts and gently eased outer edges. A memorable unmistakable silhouette, precise, calm, watchful, quietly powerful. Think an intelligent presence held in a protective precision-engineered structure, never a spinning fan or recycling arrows. No arrows or pointed arrowheads.
Color/material: deep electric sapphire blue planar vanes with restrained brushed titanium silver edge highlights; central core vivid energy cyan, with a tiny precise halo only. Very shallow dimensionality and beautiful large clean surfaces, disciplined near-flat graphic logo design. Dark midnight navy background. Form must do the work: no effects that obscure the silhouette. Premium cinematic technology identity, futuristic enough to belong to a sophisticated AI operating system, simple enough to become an app icon or physical emblem. Original visual identity, do not reproduce the Iron Man helmet, Marvel arc reactor, Avengers emblem, OpenAI knot or any existing brand.
Composition: one single final logo, front-facing orthographic, centered on a square canvas with generous margins; emblem occupies about 60 percent canvas width. No text, letters or wordmark. No chat bubble, dialogue tail, terminal window, cursor, robot face, eyes, headset, speech symbol, external-link arrow, generic star, infinity loop, atom, brain, circuit lines, nested HUD rings, little dots, technical labels, decorative lines, photorealistic object scene, pedestal, perspective, excessive chrome, lens flare, smoke, mockup, options sheet or watermark.
Prioritize a coherent visual grammar: three substantial repeated forms, one distinctive centered core, one angular construction system, extremely clear hierarchy, strong identity from the silhouette. Output a polished image-generated logo concept.
```
