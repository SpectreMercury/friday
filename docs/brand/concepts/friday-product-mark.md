# Friday Logo 设计约束与产品识别稿

## 用户累计要求

- 项目 Logo，容易传播，一眼能感知产品内容。
- 简单的机器人脸，以眼睛和嘴巴为主，黑白、有设计感。
- 帅、有科技感，眼睛和嘴巴之间要留足距离。
- 可参考钢铁侠或大白；用户提供钢铁侠面甲参考，希望有一点线条金属感。
- 表情冷漠一些。

## 本轮设计判断

产品是个人 AI 助手。以机器人助手的辨识度和小尺寸可读性为优先，金属线条作为次要细节。此前高反光金属面甲方向已被用户指出偏离 Logo 的传播和产品识别目标，不应只沿着表情和材质继续细化。

本轮用三个大块面和统一切角建立简洁面孔，金属感收敛为边缘细节。此为待评价设计稿，不代表用户已接受，也未替换当前应用图标。生成结果的表情和差异性仍需用户评价，不能将“易传播”视为已验证事实。

## 内置 imagegen 提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Design a finished iconic minimal brand mark for FRIDAY, a personal AI assistant product. This is a LOGO intended for instant recognition, easy recall and sharing, NOT an illustration of a metal face or a sci-fi movie villain. The logo must immediately read as a small intelligent ROBOT ASSISTANT. A simple original coherent face built from exactly THREE WHITE SHAPES floating on pure BLACK: two compact robotic optical eyes and one short mouth. No head outline or bounding box. Eyes: two broad compact rounded-square optical modules, width about 13% of canvas and height about 15%, aligned symmetrically, with a distinctive SINGLE clipped upper-inner corner on each, subtle engineered geometry. The other corners gently rounded. Flat level top edges, no angry diagonal eyebrows, no pupils, no frown, no squinting slits. Eye centers x=35% and x=65%, y=39%. Mouth: a short broad horizontal white slot, width 18% of canvas, height 4%, centered at y=65%, with the same slight corner treatment as eyes. Large eye-to-mouth negative space. Expression composed, impassive, quietly intelligent, never aggressive or sad. Design is 98% pure white silhouette and 2% restrained metal detail: optional hairline neutral silver bevel on lower edges only. Never reflective chrome frames or dimensional rendering. Must remain the SAME memorable symbol if those tiny highlights are removed and everything is printed in a single flat ink. Strong substantial geometry, immediate legibility at 24px, optical balance, contemporary premium software identity, iconic like a tiny robot terminal face. Pure black white and at most minimal neutral silver. No gradient body, lighting effects, glow, texture, shadows, 3D, helmet, nose, extra decorations, text or mockups. Avoid generic angry gaming eyes and movie-poster aesthetics. One final centered square logo.
