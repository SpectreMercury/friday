Use case: logo-brand.
Design one exceptional futuristic logo for a personal AI assistant. The last concept looked like a generic rounded chat bubble with an export arrow and the user said it lacked technology. Completely redesign the visual execution, preserving only the intelligible idea of an intelligent conversational terminal.
Core symbol: a compact bold angular speech-terminal glyph, a substantial nearly square open frame with a SINGLE decisive diagonal speech tail at the lower left. Inside the dark spacious aperture, place exactly ONE short upright luminous CYAN cursor bar, offset slightly right of center, like an AI terminal awake and ready. This one cursor is the identity's distinctive focal point. No eyes, face, robot head or additional dots. No arrow.
Material and color language: the main frame has a restrained satin titanium / cold silver surface with a very subtle electric indigo tint along its lower edge. Graphic-first premium industrial precision, almost flat with very shallow bevels, clean large planar highlights, no realistic scene reflections. Cyan cursor is saturated #3DF6EE with at most an extremely tight soft halo. Deep midnight navy #070B1C background. The combination should feel unmistakably advanced, intelligent, calm, and desirable. Strong readable silhouette must remain when all material treatment is removed. This is a SOFTWARE PRODUCT LOGO, not game armor or a sci-fi prop.
Composition: one centered finished symbol on a square dark navy canvas, about 58 percent canvas width, ample luxurious negative space. Front-on orthographic view, no perspective. Visually refined geometry and optical balance. Memorable enough for an app avatar and launch icon.
Avoid generic chat icons, rounded puffy bubbles, export arrows, illustration, wordmarks, text, initials, letters, circuit-board decorations, tiny lines, stars, brains, atoms, hexagon badges, elaborate shine, neon fog, lens flares, lens effects, environment, grids, excessive depth, photorealism, mockups, multiple options, caption, watermark. Exactly one logo.
