# Friday 品牌

应用名称：Friday。最终头像使用扁平圆润的 F 形符号，青色与淡紫色渐变，两枚小眼睛赋予助手的表情，深色背景适配手机页面。

通过内置 imagegen 生成，最终原图保存在 `local_ai/pwa/friday-icon.png`。使用 macOS sips 仅导出尺寸，不改变画面设计：PWA 192/512 PNG 和 Apple Touch Icon 180 PNG。页面头像与主屏幕图标来自同一张原图。

名称更新不改动站点 origin、WebAuthn RP ID、账号、Cookie 名称或会话数据库。原域名继续作为稳定登录地址。

## 最终生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final square iPhone PWA icon and AI assistant avatar for Friday.
Primary request: Completely redesign the icon as a YOUNG, FLAT, intelligent futuristic brand symbol. The user rejected the previous heavy gold metallic rings as old-fashioned. New visual direction: strikingly simple, friendly, contemporary, graphic.
Design: one distinctive small abstract AI companion glyph centered on a very dark navy-black background. A soft rounded asymmetric F-shaped monogram, drawn as a continuous broad fluid ribbon, with a clever negative-space notch. Integrate two tiny dark horizontal pill eyes in the upper bar so the monogram has a subtle alert, friendly personality without becoming a literal robot head. The silhouette must still read as an elegant lowercase f / flowing intelligence symbol. The mark should feel like a modern creative software mascot, confident and light, no toy-like 3D.
Style: PURE FLAT 2D vector-like artwork. Large simple clean shapes, precise curves, clever negative space, absolutely no metal, texture, bevels, shadows, reflections, 3D volume, or glossy orb effects. At most a restrained smooth two-color gradient inside the solid glyph from icy electric cyan to soft periwinkle; the edge is crisp, not glowing. Tiny eyes should feel like an intelligent companion rather than an emoji. No outline strokes.
Composition: centered single emblem, square image, symbol occupies approximately 58-62% of canvas width and height. Generous negative space, all important geometry inside central circular 72% safe zone. Edge-to-edge solid #080B12 background, opaque, no pre-rounded square frame.
Palette: bright clean cyan #62E6EC and cool lavender #A092FA; no gold, no orange.
Text: no written words or labels. The monogram is the only glyph.
Avoid: human face, humanoid robot, Iron Man helmet, old sci-fi HUD, rings, gears, circuit board, chat bubble, four-point AI sparkle, decorative dots outside the glyph, complex line art, generic stock logo mockup, app device mockup, multi-option sheet.
Output one polished finished icon, completely flat, perfectly readable as a small mobile app icon.
```
